T h e H a u n t e d M a z e

When darkness falls, the Sunny Acres corn maze undergoes a terrifying transformation into the Haunted Maze! Filled with live actors, spooky sound effects, special lighting, and bone-chilling surprises around every corner.

Designed for thrill-seekers and Halloween lovers of all ages, the Haunted Maze offers an unforgettable night of scares and fun. Enter if you dare!

Note: Parent discretion is advised for very young children due to dark areas and scare actors.
