P e t t i n g B a r n

Come meet our friendly farm animals at the Sunny Acres Petting Barn! We feature over 100 gentle animals including baby goats, lambs, calves, bunnies, chicks, and mini-donkeys in a clean, safe environment.

Kids and parents can bottle-feed baby animals, pet the goats, and learn all about farm life directly from our knowledgeable staff. It is an educational experience that creates lasting memories for families and school trips alike.

Hours

  • Monday - Friday: 9 am - 4 pm
  • Saturday & Sunday: 11 am - 3 pm

Special Guidelines

  • Hand sanitizing stations are provided at the exit.
  • Please call ahead for large school groups or birthday parties.
