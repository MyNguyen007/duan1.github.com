T h e C o r n M a z e

Every year Sunny Acres transforms its cornfield into a giant maze. The maze is open during weekends in September and October, and on Halloween night. Each year features a new layout, so even if you've visited before, you'll find a new challenge awaiting you.

Our maze spans over 10 acres of tall corn with twisty turnings, dead ends, and hidden checkpoints. Don't worry if you get lost — we have "Corn Copters" on standby and helpful guides throughout the maze to lend a hand!

It's great fun for groups, families, or anyone looking to test their navigation skills. Be sure to dress comfortably and wear sturdy shoes for walking in the field.

Maze Hours

  • Saturdays: 11 am - 9 pm
  • Sundays: 11 am - 5 pm
  • Halloween Night: 5 pm - 10 pm

Admission Fees

  • Adults (12+): $10.00
  • Children (4 - 11): $6.00
  • Under 4: Free
